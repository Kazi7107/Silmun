Two kinds of agents

With or without an AI Agent Studio license.

Kind AFor teams with an AI Agent Studio license

Built in AI Agent Studio and runs on ServiceNow's own LLMs, under the Now Assist entitlement you already pay for. It shows up in AI Control Tower alongside your other agents and runs in supervised mode.

How we build

Agents your security team can sign off on.

The same three rules hold for every agent we ship.

01

Human-in-the-loop on every write.

Agents stage changes. An approver confirms anything that changes ownership, risk posture or your catalog, through the platform approval engine.

Staged→Requested→Approved
02

Everything is journaled.

Every action is written to the work notes of the record it touched, with its reasoning and sources, so it stays in the audit history.
