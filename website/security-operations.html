Security Operations Agent for ServiceNow

Vulnerability Remediation

ServiceNow already finds and assigns vulnerabilities. But fixes still get stuck: nobody owns the server, the fix needs three teams, the patch can't go in yet, or two systems disagree. This agent is designed to dig into those cases, line up the next step and check the fix worked. A person signs off on anything that matters.

What it does

Eight problems it's built for.

All eight are live now.

  • 01No ownerFinds who should fix it and asks them to confirm.
  • 02Many teamsPlans a fix that needs several teams, in the right order.
  • 03Patch blockedFinds a safe workaround when you can't patch yet.
  • 04No patch yetPrepares the paperwork to accept the risk for now.
  • 05Records disagreeSorts out scan results and asset records that don't match.
  • 06Stalled taskFinds out why a fix is late, not just that it's late.
  • 07Patched, still openChecks why a patched item still shows as open.
  • 08Unknown hardwareWorks out what an unlabeled device is and who owns it.
